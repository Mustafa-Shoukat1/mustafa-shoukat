# Business Owner Discovery Questions

A short, business-first conversation before recommending AI or automation. Saved 16 Oct 2026 from Mustafa's notes.

## Questions to ask

1. "If you had to step away from the business for two weeks, what would start falling apart?"
2. "What happens between someone becoming interested in your business and actually becoming a customer?"
3. "Where do things usually get stuck in that process?"
4. "What do you or your team keep checking, updating, copying, or following up on every day?"
5. "When a lead comes in at the wrong time, after hours, during a busy period, or when nobody is available, what happens?"
6. "What's something your team does today that you feel shouldn't require a person to do every single time?"
7. "If your business suddenly doubled next year, what part of the operation would break first?"
8. "What have you been putting off fixing because it works 'well enough' for now?"
9. "What information does your team need to make decisions, but is difficult or slow to get?"
10. "If I watched your team work for one full day, where do you think I'd see the biggest waste of time?"

Killer question: "What are you doing manually today simply because 'that's how we've always done it'?"

## The most important question

"If we forget about AI for a moment, what problem would you really want us to solve?"

This moves the conversation away from tools and toward the actual business problem.

## Core principle

Understand the problem, identify the opportunity, then recommend the right solution. Do not sell a tool first. Find out what the business actually needs.

Daily Operations Checklist — Codagentic Agency
================================================

1. ATTRACTION
   - Do I need to reply to any client today?
   - Do I need to follow up with someone today?

2. RETENTION
   - Is any client not getting expected results?
   - Do I need to proactively check in?
   - Are we actually hitting weekly targets?

3. TEAM
   - Do I need to hire someone today?
   - Or do I need to change someone's role?

4. SYSTEMS
   - What am I repeatedly fixing?
   - How do I systemize this so it stops needing me?

5. GROWTH
   - Are my Upwork profiles still ranking?
   - Can I tighten positioning to attract better clients?
   - Do I need to add a skill that expands outcomes, not workload?

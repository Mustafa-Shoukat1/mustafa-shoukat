# Job market (employed) vs freelance/consulting demand for AI, RAG, data and automation skills in real estate and PropTech (US, UK, remote)

Research date: 12 October 2026. Method: 44 web searches (the shared budget then ran out) plus direct fetches of known URLs. **[snippet-only]** marks facts taken from search-result summaries where the page itself could not be fetched; **[self-reported]** marks Levels.fyi / Indeed user submissions; **[vendor]** marks a vendor's own marketing claim. Unreachable pages are listed in each section's Gaps and collected at the end.

## Key question 1: Titles and counts for the target searches on LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, Otta, Wellfound, Built In and PropTech boards

### Takeaway
Public LinkedIn count pages show "11,000+" US and "8,000+" UK results for PropTech-style keywords, but LinkedIn's keyword matching is loose (its "Follow Up Boss" page is mostly collections and customer-service jobs), so these are ceilings, not role counts. The only clean count series found is IT Jobs Watch (UK): 137 permanent ads citing "PropTech" in the six months to 11 Oct 2026 (17 a year earlier), 808 citing "AI Engineer" (91 a year earlier), 505 citing "RAG" (84), 2,087 citing "Data Engineer" (637). Indeed, Glassdoor, ZipRecruiter and most individual job pages returned 403; Otta and Unissu could not be checked; Proptech Connect has no jobs board.

### Inferences
- Counts for the exact phrases requested ("AI engineer real estate", "Follow Up Boss consultant", "AppFolio consultant", "Reapit consultant", "real estate automation specialist") are not retrievable as clean numbers from public pages: LinkedIn's "11,000+" headers include every job that mentions any of the words. The only defensible numeric series is UK IT Jobs Watch, where PropTech-tagged ads are small (137 in six months, mostly sales titles) while generic AI Engineer and RAG ads grew roughly 9x and 6x year on year.
- In the US, the named employers that actually post AI/data engineering titles (from the LinkedIn samples) are AppFolio, Zillow, CoStar/Homes.com/Apartments.com, Realtor.com, Crexi, EliseAI, JLL, First American and Compass; Keller Williams, eXp, Lone Wolf and Inside Real Estate did not appear in any sample.
- In the UK, PropTech engineering ads cluster in London and Manchester; the employers that appear are portals and CRMs (Rightmove, Houseful, Reapit, Street Group, Yardi, CoStar UK) and a long tail of small start-ups.

## Part C: Comparison of employed roles (A) and freelance/consulting (B) on income, time to first revenue, competition, real-estate background or licence, and overlapping skills

### Takeaway
On the evidence gathered, employed PropTech AI/data roles pay $126K–$290K median total comp in the US and £65K–£87.5K median in the UK, are mostly hybrid/on-site, and are concentrated at a dozen employers that have been cutting or consolidating; freelance demand from small real-estate businesses is visible but priced at $10–$35 per gig, $5–$10/hr offshore and $25–$75/hr for Python/automation builds, with 50+ bidders per Upwork post. Neither path shows a licence requirement in the sources found; the overlapping skills are Python, LLM/RAG/agentic workflows, CRM configuration (HubSpot/Follow Up Boss/kvCORE/Lofty/AppFolio), Zapier/Make/n8n, SQL and pipelines, and MLS/RESO data handling.

### Inferences
- The employed path's income ceiling is set by the stock-paying portals (Zillow, Opendoor, Redfin) which are the same firms that cut 2–7% of staff in 2025–2026; the base-heavy property-management vendors (AppFolio, RealPage, Entrata, Yardi) pay $100K–$212K and were not reported cutting.
- The freelance path's public price floor is set by Fiverr and Philippine VAs, so a US/UK-priced consultant's revenue depends on work that buyers already price at $25–$150/hr (custom automation, data, AI) rather than CRM setup, and on private channels not measured here (LinkedIn Services, referrals, vendor partner directories), for which no data was found.
- Neither path requires a licence on the evidence; both reward MLS/RESO, CRM and compliance domain knowledge.

# Job market (employed roles) vs freelance/consulting demand for legal AI, RAG and workflow automation (US, UK, remote) — research notes, 11 Oct 2026

Scope: (A) employed roles for the skill set "AI engineer / RAG engineer / legal automation / practice-management consultant (Clio, Filevine, LEAP, Actionstep) / legal-ops technologist / legal engineer"; (B) freelance and consulting demand from small law firms; (C) comparison on the stated criteria. No recommendation is made.

Source labelling used throughout:
- **[search snippet]** = fact taken from a search-result summary; the underlying page was not opened (several sites returned HTTP 403 to a direct fetch: Upwork, Fiverr, americanbar.org, clio.com/blog, Glassdoor UK, JD Journal, lawtechpartners.com, cpn-legal.com).
- **[vendor / self-reported]** = the number comes from the seller, consultancy or vendor's own page or marketing.
- Unlabelled facts were read from the fetched page.

## Key question 3: Required skills and experience (incl. JD / law degree)

### Takeaway
Two distinct profiles appear: (1) "legal engineer" = JD plus 3+ years practice, presentation and executive skills, coding not required; (2) "AI engineer" at legal-tech vendors/firms = Python, production LLM work, NLP, with RAG, vector/graph DBs, agents/MCP as pluses and 6–8+ years' experience, with no legal-domain requirement stated. UK law-firm "legal technology specialist" adverts cluster around Harvey AI, HighQ, Legatics, SharePoint, Smartsheet and generative AI rather than engineering stacks.

### Cited Findings
- Harvey Legal Engineer (NY) requires "JD or equivalent legal qualification"; "At least 3 years of experience practicing law at a top-tier law firm (Vault 50 or equivalent) and/or in-house experience"; "Executive engagement skills and presence"; "Outstanding presentation skills to both legal and executive audiences"; "Strong understanding of legal processes". Technical skills: none listed. Pluses: curiosity about AI, sales/customer-facing experience, managing law-firm client matters. — [Legal.io](https://www.legal.io/jobs/5787729/Full-time/Legal-Engineer/Remote)
- Harvey postings "generally ask for 3+ years of practicing law in-house, at a top-tier law firm, or advising a sophisticated corporate legal team." **[search snippet]** — [Harvey careers](https://www.harvey.ai/company/careers/f4d70ed0-010e-40f5-b194-2813e55f6e0c)
- "Coding is not required" for legal engineers; they need to understand how LLMs and the platform work; Luminance fills legal-analyst roles with recent law graduates and some postings say "qualified lawyers are strongly favoured"; legal engineer, customer success and implementation roles require more experience (19 Feb 2026). — [NonBillable](https://www.nonbillable.co.uk/posts/legal-careers-big-law-ai-working-at-harvey-legora-luminance)

## Key question 4: Remote share and openness to non-US / non-UK candidates; visa language

### Takeaway
Across all US AI-engineering postings only 32% are fully remote (48% hybrid, 20% on-site), and the legal-tech vendor roles sampled are mostly hybrid/on-site in NY/SF/London; no "legal engineer" posting with visa-sponsorship language was found, and Harvey's own posting is silent on work authorization. Vendors do hire outside the US/UK, but in their own offshore offices (Bratislava, Bengaluru, Pune, Berlin, Stockholm, Zurich).

### Inferences
- Income: the employed path's floor in the US ($150K base at vendors) exceeds the consultancy-tier ceiling a solo consultant could reach at 10–15 billable hours/week on the cited rates; the UK employed floor (£42K) is below what a UK freelancer at £80–£200/hr would gross at the same utilisation. These are arithmetic comparisons on cited figures, not reported outcomes.
- Competition is structurally different: employed roles are few (hundreds of engineering postings sector-wide, concentrated in ~5 vendors) and screened on 6–8+ years and, for legal-engineer titles, a JD; freelance categories are priced down by marketplace sellers at $20–$61/hr, with the protected niches being vendor-certified programs (Clio CCC ~100, Filevine certified partners, LEAP UK partners).
- Law background: a hard requirement only for the "legal engineer" title; absent from AI-engineer ads and from freelance buyer vetting advice, which asks for law-firm project history and confidentiality handling instead.

# Filevine vs Clio gap map for small and mid-size US law firms (1-30 lawyers), with a Clio UK vs LEAP note

Research date: 11 Oct 2026. Labels used below: [vendor] = vendor marketing or vendor help page; [user] = named or dated user review; [3p] = third-party analyst, aggregator or consultant page. Access notes: reddit.com is blocked for this crawler (both search and fetch), and clio.com, capterra.com, getapp.com, softwareadvice.com, g2.com, support.filevine.com, support.leaddocket.com, help.clio.com (most articles), upwork.com job pages and fiverr.com gig pages all returned HTTP 403, so facts from those sites come from search-engine snippets of the cited URL, not from a full page read. Where only a snippet was available this is stated.

## Q1. Who buys each product (firm size, practice areas)

### Takeaway
Filevine's base is plaintiff-side PI and mass-tort firms that want a configurable, phase-driven system and can absorb a partner-led 4-12 week implementation; Clio Manage's base is general solo-to-mid-size practice across practice areas, bought for billing, time, documents and ease of setup, with a PI add-on bolted on since Oct 2023.

### Cited Findings
- Filevine has "historically" sold to "mid-sized plaintiff firms" with a focus on "high-volume plaintiff practices", with mass torts and criminal defense as adjacent segments; about 3,400 customers mid-2024, "nearly 6,000" by Sep 2025, ARR estimated at $250M in Apr 2026, net dollar retention above 120% [3p] — [Sacra, May 2026](https://sacra.com/c/filevine)
- Lawyerist: "Personal-injury firms and other process-oriented practices will likely find the best results with Filevine"; it "needs more setup effort than many competitors" and is "probably more than solo practices need" [3p] — [Lawyerist Filevine review](https://lawyerist.com/reviews/law-practice-management-software/filevine/)
- Dupple (updated May 2026) says Filevine is not for "solo and 2-3 attorney firms starting out (the page suggests Clio)", "firms with limited time for onboarding", or "primarily transactional practices with short-lived matters"; implementation is "a multi-week project (4-12 weeks) requiring partners" [3p] — [Dupple Filevine review](https://dupple.com/reviews/filevine)
- A comparison site says Filevine "can suit personal injury, mass tort, complex litigation, and larger teams with dedicated operations staff" and to "expect 4 to 8 weeks of implementation" [3p] — [The Digital Project Manager, Clio vs Filevine 2026](https://thedigitalprojectmanager.com/tools/clio-vs-filevine/)
- TrustRadius Filevine reviewers in Aug-Sep 2026 are mostly PI (firm sizes 1-10, 11-50, 51-200) plus litigation and a 501-1000 firm [user] — [TrustRadius Filevine reviews](https://web-v2.prod.trustradius.com/products/filevine/reviews/all)
- Lawyerist: "Clio Manage is a good fit for most solo to medium-sized firms"; firms wanting plug-and-play "may want to review something more than just Clio Manage" [3p] — [Lawyerist Clio review](https://lawyerist.com/reviews/law-practice-management-software/clio/)
- Clio crossed "$500M ARR in May 2026", serves "hundreds of thousands of legal professionals in 130+ countries", and is moving upmarket with Clio Operate (ShareDo acquisition, launched in North America Mar 2026) [3p] — [Sacra, May 2026](https://sacra.com/c/filevine)
- Clio introduced a Personal Injury add-on to Clio Manage on 9 Oct 2023 "specifically designed for a PI lawyer's distinct workflows" [3p reporting a vendor launch] — [LawNext, 9 Oct 2023](https://www.lawnext.com/2023/10/clio-goes-all-out-with-major-product-announcements-including-a-personal-injury-add-on-e-filing-and-of-course-generative-ai.html)
- Filevine's own case study: Connecticut Trial Firm, 10 staff, single-event PI, moved from "Dropbox and Clio" [vendor] — [Filevine case study](https://filevine.com/customers/case-studies/connecticut-trial-firm/)
- Public-sector buyers also exist: Marin County, CA signed a 3-year Filevine subscription at $67,944/yr (term to 30 Jun 2029, no auto-renew); Clinton County, NY signed a 3-year $149,856 public-defender case-management contract (snippet only) [3p] — [CivicIQ Marin](https://civiciq.com/public-contract/cbcdbbcd-a682-4543-8213-34422ff206e5); [CivicIQ Clinton](https://civiciq.com/public-contract/f4508b41-a219-4470-8a6e-eaeca3415da5)

### Inferences
- Filevine's buyer in the 1-30 lawyer band is a PI/litigation firm with an office manager or ops person who will own configuration; Clio's buyer is any small firm that wants billing and matters working in days. The overlap zone (5-30 lawyer PI firms) is where the switching stories concentrate.

### Gaps
- No independent customer-count split by firm size for either vendor; Sacra's numbers are estimates.

## Q2. Pricing tiers and real total cost including add-ons

### Takeaway
Clio publishes a starting price (about $49/user/month) and hides the rest behind "contact sales" since Mar 2026, with Grow, Manage AI (ex-Duo), PI add-on, payments fees, migration and training as extras; Filevine publishes nothing, bills per seat plus per-project AI, usage-based automations and separately priced Lead Docket, Vinesign, Outlaw and partner implementation, so a 15-user firm's 3-year cost has been estimated at $33k-$64k before AI and intake add-ons.

### Cited Findings (Clio)
- Price tracker snapshot 8 Jun 2026: EasyStart $49, Essentials $89, Advanced $119 per user/month (annual); Expand (ex-Complete) "Contact"; Clio Grow add-on "$59/mo, billed annually"; Clio Manage AI (Duo) add-on and Clio for Personal Injury add-on both "Contact" [3p] — [PricingSaaS Clio](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/clio)
- Dated changes logged by the same tracker: Q2 2025 EasyStart $39 to $49 (25.6%), Essentials $79 to $89 (12.7%), Complete $139 to $149 (7.2%); Q3 2025 summer 25% first-year discount removed; 23 Mar 2026 Essentials/Advanced/Expand prices hidden behind contact CTAs; 5 Aug 2026 tiers renamed Starter / Core / Signature / Elite with Starter still $49 [3p] — [PricingSaaS Clio](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/clio); [PricingSaaS 2025Q2 diff](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/clio/diffs/2025Q2)

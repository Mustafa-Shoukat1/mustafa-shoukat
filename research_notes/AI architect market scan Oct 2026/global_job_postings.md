### Inferences
- The Forward Deployed Engineer label has absorbed much of what used to be called "AI Solutions Architect" at product companies: 13 of the 30 in-window rows carry an FDE or "forward deployed" title, and their requirement lists (customer-facing design plus production code) match the architect brief.
- Pure "Enterprise AI Architect" and "GenAI Architect" titles are concentrated in large enterprises, consultancies and UK contract agencies, which is consistent with the Axial sector split.
- Postings surface and close fast in the UK contract market: both freehire contract rows opened and closed within 15 to 19 days.

### Inferences
- The candidate's core stack (RAG plus agents) matches the two most common technical asks, but half of the detailed postings do not name RAG at all; "agents" is now the more universal keyword, with RAG as one component.
- MCP has moved from novelty to a mainstream requirement in under a year: 10 of 28 postings name it, including Anthropic, Google, SAP, Datadog and Tiger Analytics.
- Azure is the most-named single cloud in both my sample (10 of 28) and UK contract data (38.53%), which matters for enterprise and Gulf-adjacent work.
- Kubernetes is close to absent in architect and FDE postings. It probably sits in the AI Platform Engineer family, which my sample under-represents, so do not read 1 of 28 as "Kubernetes does not matter".

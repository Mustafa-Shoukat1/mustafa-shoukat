## Key Question 1: 2026 hourly rate percentiles for AI/LLM engineers on Upwork (P50, P90) and on Toptal / Gun.io / Braintrust / A.Team (plus Contra, Arc)

### Takeaway
No platform publishes true P50/P90 for "AI engineer". The best triangulation for Upwork in 2026 is a median around $100/hour for ML/LLM engineers with a wide $25 to $250 spread; specialist RAG/agent work on Upwork is quoted at $120 to $250/hour by multiple 2026 guides, while the typical "AI engineer" listing clusters at $35 to $60. Toptal and Gun.io AI/ML bands sit at roughly $100 to $250/hour billed to the client, with freelancer take-home reduced by 10 to 50 percent depending on platform.

## Key Question 2: What enterprise RAG and agentic builds sell for as fixed-price projects in 2026, and what retainers (fractional AI architect / advisory) and AI audits cost

### Takeaway
Three distinct price layers exist in 2026: marketplace gigs (RAG knowledge base $800 to $3,500; agent setup $1,200 to $6,000), independent/boutique pilots and production builds ($10k to $50k pilot; $50k to $150k production; €80k to €250k for a European enterprise rollout), and firm-led enterprise programmes ($150k to $500k+). Advisory retainers run $2k to $15k/month for independents; fractional AI architect/CAIO retainers $7k to $40k/month; LLM security/system audits $6k to $50k for specialists.

It is, however, 3 to 10x above marketplace gig tickets for the same labels ("RAG knowledge base", "agent setup").
- Independent consultants selling to enterprises in 2026 anchor on phased scoping (~€8k / $5k to $25k) leading to a prototype (€25k to 60k / $10k to $50k) and then production; this staged structure is how a solo practitioner reaches five-figure and six-figure deals without quoting a single large number upfront.
- Retainers for AI advisory are mostly $2k to $15k/month for independents; the $15k+ tiers require either a team or fractional-CAIO positioning with regulated-industry governance scope.

### Gaps
- No source gave a statistically grounded distribution of actual closed fixed-price RAG deals; all figures are vendor or consultant guides.
- No fixed-price benchmarks specifically for Arabic-language or Saudi-domiciled RAG builds were found.

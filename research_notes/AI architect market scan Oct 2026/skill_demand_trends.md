## Key Question 1: What do Indeed Hiring Lab, LinkedIn, Lightcast, Stack Overflow 2026, Gartner, McKinsey, Deloitte, WEF and similar 2026 reports say about demand for each of the 12 skill areas?

### Takeaway
The 2026 labour-market sources agree on direction but rarely measure at the level of individual skills: AI mentions in job postings roughly doubled in a year, "agentic AI" is the single fastest-growing measured skill cluster (+280%, about 86,000 to 90,000 US postings, median $153K), and demand has shifted from talking to models toward deploying, governing and paying for them. Hard posting counts exist for agents, AI governance and AI architecture; for RAG, MCP, LLM evaluation, Arabic NLP and cloud-by-vendor the evidence is adoption surveys and market estimates, not job-posting data.

### Inferences
Durability judgements below are the researcher's reading of the cited evidence, not statements made by the sources.
- **RAG / retrieval: high demand, flattening as a differentiator.** It is the default customisation route (57% do not fine-tune; 51% adoption already in 2024), so it is assumed, not rewarded on its own. Durable only when paired with evaluation, domain data and language expertise.
- **Agent orchestration: rising fastest, with a correction risk.** Strongest measured growth and a pay premium, but Gartner places it at peak hype and developers prefer single agents. Durable for those who can run constrained, supervised agents in production; fragile for demo-level multi-agent work.
- **MCP / tool integration: rising, likely to commoditise quickly.** Fast adoption and vendor-neutral governance make it table stakes; the scarce part is securing and governing it, not wiring it.
- **Evaluation / golden datasets: rising and scarce.** Quality is the top production blocker and roughly 30% run no evaluation; a gap between 89% observability and 52% offline evals marks where the skill shortage sits.
- **Observability / AgentOps: rising from table stakes among agent teams to mainstream.** 14% production use among general observability teams versus Gartner's 50% by 2028 implies several years of demand.
- **Kubernetes / on-prem / sovereign: steady and durable, stronger in regulated and Gulf settings.** Kubernetes is established infrastructure; the growth is in running inference on it under residency constraints.
- **Cloud vendor skill: flat as a differentiator.** AWS still leads share and posting mentions, Google is gaining, and multi-model use is the norm, so portability matters more than one vendor badge.
- **Cost / FinOps for LLMs: rising and under-supplied.** Near-universal tracking, low real-time visibility, and "pre-deployment architecture costing" named as a wanted capability.
- **Security / governance / HITL: rising and durable.** Governance maturity (21%) lags intended agent use (74%), and governance roles are senior-weighted and well paid.
- **Prompt engineering: falling as a title, absorbed as a baseline skill.** Still matters economically inside systems (system prompts are 69% of input tokens) but is not a standalone identity.
- **Arabic / multilingual NLP: scarce by supply-side evidence, unmeasured by demand-side data.** State investment is large; no posting statistics exist to quantify the premium.
- **Architecture judgement: rising and the most durable.** The rebound in developer hiring is senior-weighted, architecture roles are the highest-volume AI function in one recruiter dataset, and high performers are distinguished by workflow redesign.

# Search language, job ads and freelance gigs: small dental practices UK and US

Research date: 16 October 2026. Method: web search (budget exhausted after the first batch), direct fetches of job boards and freelance marketplaces (WebFetch, then curl with a browser user agent where WebFetch was refused: Upwork, Fiverr, Indeed UK, Indeed US and NHS Jobs all returned HTTP 200 that way), and the Google autocomplete endpoint (suggestqueries.google.com, US and UK locales, fetched with delays after rate limiting). Problem and language research only; no tools recommended.

## Key Question 1: Most frequent duties and pay in small-practice ads (UK: Reed, Indeed UK, NHS Jobs, BDJ Jobs; US: Indeed, ZipRecruiter, LinkedIn, DentalPost, Craigslist)

### Takeaway
US ads use a rich coordinator vocabulary (treatment, patient care, insurance/financial, scheduling, hygiene, new-patient, authorizations) and pay $18 to $35 an hour for front office and $50,000 to $65,000 for office managers; Indeed's own duty tags across 44 US cards rank "Medical scheduling", "Greeting dental patients", "Dental insurance", "Managing patient records", "Medical insurance coverage verification" and "Patient collections management" highest. UK ads use "dental receptionist", "dental nurse" and "practice manager"; Indeed's tags across 15 UK cards rank "Patient service", "Patient appointment coordination", "Handling customer enquiries", "Inbound customer enquiry call handling", "Record keeping" and "SOE Exact" highest, at £12.71 to £14 an hour or roughly £24,000 to £28,000 a year, while practice manager ads lead with CQC, GDC, HTM 01-05 and NHS compliance at £28,000 to £40,000.

### Inferences
- In the US, "insurance" is the organising duty: 14 of 44 Indeed cards carry "Medical insurance coverage verification", 11 carry "Patient collections management" and 8 "Medical claims submission"; ads bundle verification, claims, authorizations and collections into whichever front-office title is used, and pay rises with insurance-heavy and specialty roles (NYC $30 to $45, Seattle $25 to $40).
- In the UK, no Indeed card carries an insurance or claims tag; the receptionist's job is enquiries, bookings, records and NHS/private forms, with "SOE Exact" the one named system (4 of 15 cards). Treatment-plan explanation ("discuss treatment plans", "extensive knowledge of dental treatment types") is the UK equivalent of the US treatment coordinator function.
- UK pay in small practices is hourly and low (£12.71 to £14), with salaried posts in corporate groups and NHS community services (£24,000 to £28,000; Band 2 £25,272); NHS Jobs is a community/salaried dental channel, not a high-street small-practice board.
- Reed's keyword search is noisy, so Reed counts (94, 145, 288) overstate dental admin vacancies; Indeed's 604 (UK) and 8,842 (US) and DentalPost's 326 are cleaner signals.

### Gaps
- Full ad bodies on Reed (cards carry no snippets), BDJ Jobs (challenge page), findajob.dwp.gov.uk (503), jobs24 (410 Gone) and Rodericks careers (domain blocked) could not be read; UK duty counts rely on Indeed's 15 tagged cards plus search summaries.
- Indeed cards show one or two snippet bullets, not full duty lists; the attribute counts are Indeed's own extraction, not mine.
- ZipRecruiter, CareerBuilder and icims pages returned 403/410; Dentistry.co.uk/jobs returned a 2013 news article.
- No UK pay data for treatment coordinator, insurance/billing coordinator or marketing coordinator titles; UK autocomplete shows "treatment coordinator dental salary uk per hour" and "dental tco salary" as live questions.

## Key Question 2: Tasks practices and dental agencies outsource on Upwork, Fiverr, PeoplePerHour and OnlineJobs.ph, budgets, and what the posts reveal

### Takeaway
Upwork holds hundreds of open dental posts (276 for "dental practice", 361 for "dental marketing", 150 for "dental chatbot OR dental ai", 77 "dental receptionist", 61 "dental virtual assistant", 55 "dental billing", 43 "dental insurance verification"); the practice-placed ones are US revenue-cycle admin (verification, breakdown of benefits, pre-authorizations, claims, attachments, AR/denials, EOB posting, patient statements), remote front desk with 8am to 8pm coverage, lead follow-up with pay per booked consultation, and local SEO/website/social work, while a second cluster is AI-receptionist vendors hiring commission closers, including one "AI Receptionist for UK Dental Practices ... missed and after-hours calls". OnlineJobs.ph pays $2.50 to $8 an hour for the same admin tasks.
